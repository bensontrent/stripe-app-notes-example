## What is free

- **Test mode is always free.** Try everything on test data for as long as
  you like.
- **Every Stripe account gets a free trial in live mode:** 100 notes or 90
  days, whichever comes first. The trial is shared by everyone who uses the
  app in your account, and it starts when someone starts it in the app, not
  when the app is installed.

## When the trial ends

Nothing is lost. You can still read, edit, resolve and delete every note
and task you have. To **add new notes** you need a plan.

## Subscribing

{% steps %}

{% step title="Open the plans" %}
In the app, follow the link to the plans, or go to [Plans](/plans).
{% /step %}

{% step title="Create an account and subscribe" %}
Subscribing needs an account on this site, so there is someone to send
receipts to. Payment is handled by Stripe Checkout.
{% /step %}

{% step title="Log in from the app" %}
Back in the Stripe Dashboard, choose **Log in** in the app and then
**Recheck my plan**. The app unlocks for everyone in your Stripe account.
{% /step %}

{% /steps %}

{% callout type="info" title="Only the person paying needs an account" %}
Your teammates use the app without logging in anywhere. One plan covers
every Stripe account its owner logs in from.
{% /callout %}

## Managing your plan

Change plan, update your card or cancel at any time from your
[billing page](/billing). Uninstalling the app does not cancel a plan.
