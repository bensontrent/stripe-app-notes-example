Notetaskerator is a Stripe App for support teams. It puts a notes panel on
every customer, invoice and payment in your Stripe Dashboard, and gives your
team one shared queue of the things that still need doing.

## What you can do

- **Write a note** on a customer, an invoice or a payment, so the next person
  who opens that page knows what happened.
- **Turn a note into a task** when it needs follow-up: give it a priority and
  assign it to a teammate.
- **Work the queue.** The full-page view lists every open task, most urgent
  first, with filters for what is assigned to you and what nobody has picked
  up yet.

{% callout title="Everyone on the team sees the same notes" %}
Notes belong to your Stripe account, not to the person who wrote them.
Everyone who uses the app in your account can read, edit, resolve and delete
every note.
{% /callout %}

## Where to find it

| In the Stripe Dashboard | What the app shows |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ |
| A customer's page | Every note about that customer, including notes written on their invoices and payments |
| An invoice's page | The notes on that invoice |
| A payment's page | The notes on that payment |
| The app's own page | The task queue for the whole team |

On a customer, invoice or payment page, open the app from the Dashboard's app
drawer. The task queue is the app's full-page view.

## Test mode and live mode

Notes written in test mode stay in test mode, and notes written in live mode
stay in live mode. Test mode is always free.
