## Writing a note

{% steps %}

{% step title="Open a customer, invoice or payment" %}
Go to its page in the Stripe Dashboard and open Notetaskerator from the app
drawer.
{% /step %}

{% step title="Write the note" %}
Type what happened and choose **Add note**. The note is saved with your name
and the time.
{% /step %}

{% step title="Make it a task, if it needs follow-up" %}
Switch on **This needs follow-up** before saving. Choose a priority and,
if you know who should handle it, an assignee.
{% /step %}

{% /steps %}

A note you only want on record stays a plain note. You can turn it into a
task later, and turn a task back into a plain note.

## Tasks

A task is a note with three extra things:

| | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Priority** | Low, Normal, High, Urgent |
| **Status** | Open, In progress, Resolved |
| **Assignee** | A teammate, or nobody |

Anyone on the team can change any of them, on the page the note was written
on or from the task queue.

## Assigning a task

You can assign a task to anyone in your Stripe account **who has opened the
app at least once**. Stripe does not tell apps who is on your team, so the
app learns about a teammate the first time they open it.

{% callout type="info" title="Someone missing from the list?" %}
Ask them to open Notetaskerator once, on any customer, invoice or payment
page, or the task queue. They appear in the assignee list straight away.
{% /callout %}

When you assign a task to someone else, they get an email with the note, its
priority and a link to the page it is on. Each person can switch these
emails off with **Email me when a task is assigned to me** in the task queue.

## The task queue

The app's full-page view lists the team's tasks. By default it shows what is
open or in progress, most urgent first and then oldest first.

- **Assigned to me** shows your own tasks.
- **Unassigned** shows tasks nobody has picked up.
- **Everyone** shows the whole team's.

Choose a different status to see resolved tasks. Select a task to read the
full note, change it, or jump to the customer, invoice or payment it is
about.

## A customer's page shows everything

A note on an invoice or a payment also appears on the page of the customer
it belongs to, so support staff who start from the customer see the whole
story in one place.

## Editing and deleting

Everyone in your Stripe account can edit and delete every note. Deleting
cannot be undone.
